Common Questions
How long does it take to get a reply?
We aim to reply to all general inquiries within 24 hours. Support tickets are prioritized and typically resolved within 4 hours during business hours.
I found a bug in PathPilot. How do I report it?
Use the form above and select "Report a Bug" from the subject dropdown. Detailed bug reports (screenshots, steps to reproduce) help us fix issues much faster.
Can I request a feature or suggest an improvement?
Absolutely — we love user-driven product development. Select "Feature Request" in the form and describe what you need. The best features have come from our users.
I'm interested in integrating PathPilot into my platform.
We have a developer API and partnership program. Reach out via our Partnerships email or select "Partnership Inquiry" in the form above and we'll set up a call.

FAQ

Common Questions

How does the resume analysis work?
+
Upload your PDF resume and our AI engine extracts every skill, technology, role, and achievement. It then scores your ATS compatibility, keyword density, and readability — giving you actionable fixes within seconds.
Is my resume data private and secure?
+
Yes. Your resume is processed securely and is never shared with employers or third parties. You own your data, and you can delete it at any time from your account settings.
How accurate is the job match score?
+
Our matching algorithm achieves 95% accuracy when benchmarked against real hiring outcomes. It analyzes hard skills, soft skills, experience levels, and role-specific keywords to produce a granular match score.
Can I analyze multiple job descriptions?
+
Absolutely. The Smart Job Analyzer lets you paste any job description from any source and instantly evaluate your eligibility. You can analyze as many roles as you want and track your progress over time.
What career paths does the AI roadmap cover?
+
PathPilot generates roadmaps for 120+ career paths including AI Engineer, Data Scientist, ML Engineer, DevOps, Full-Stack Developer, Cloud Architect, and more — with new paths added regularly based on market demand.
Is PathPilot AI free to use?
+
Yes, PathPilot offers a free tier that includes resume analysis, basic skill gap detection, and market trends overview. Advanced features like the full roadmap generator and unlimited job matching are available in the Pro plan.

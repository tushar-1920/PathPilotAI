FAQ

Common Questions

How does the resume analysis work?
+
Upload your PDF resume and our AI engine extracts every skill, technology, role, and achievement. It then scores your ATS compatibility, keyword density, and readability — giving you actionable fixes within seconds.
What is the AI Interview Simulator?
+
A full real-interview simulation with 5 types — HR, Technical, ML, Coding, and System Design. The AI asks questions, speaks them aloud via TTS, you answer by voice (Whisper transcription) or text, and it scores you 0-10 with strengths, improvements, and a full final report.
What is VidCode and how does it work?
+
VidCode is a live coding interview platform combining video call (WebRTC), Monaco editor (same as VS Code), live code execution with stdin, and real-time sync via Socket.IO — all in one room. Host creates a room, shares the 6-digit code, participants join from any device on the same network.
Is my resume data private and secure?
+
Yes. Your resume is processed securely and is never shared with employers or third parties. You own your data, and you can delete it at any time from your account settings.
Can I analyze multiple job descriptions?
+
Absolutely. The Smart Job Analyzer lets you paste any job description from any source and instantly evaluate your eligibility. Analyze as many roles as you want and track your progress over time.
What career paths does the AI roadmap cover?
+
PathPilot generates roadmaps for 120+ career paths including AI Engineer, Data Scientist, ML Engineer, DevOps, Full-Stack Developer, Cloud Architect, and more — with new paths added regularly based on market demand.
Is PathPilot AI free to use?
+
Yes, all 12 features are available to registered users — resume analysis, skill gap detection, market trends, interview simulator, VidCode, coding practice, profile system, messaging, and more. Free to start, no credit card required.
